# Third-party data notice

The files in this directory whose names contain `padt`, and the files derived
from them (the `probe_baseline_*` stimuli and reports that carry
`sentence_text`), include sentences and annotations from the
[UD Arabic-PADT treebank](https://universaldependencies.org/treebanks/ar_padt),
which is based on the Prague Arabic Dependency Treebank (Charles University,
Prague; mostly newswire text).
